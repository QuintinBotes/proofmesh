# Provenance

Project-authored for Proofmesh. Apache-2.0. No third-party code. The EF Core, ASP.NET Core and hosting types are minimal stand-ins declared in the fixture. Three entities map to tables by the DbSet convention: an endpoint reaches `Orders` through a repository, a hosted-service job reaches `Shipments`, and nothing reaches `Archive`. `observations.json` (schema 0.1) and `property-reads.json` (schema 0.2: `Order.Id` and `Order.Total` read by the orders endpoint, and a `Discount` the entity does not have) and `property-reads-0.3.json` (schema 0.3: `Id` loaded by the ORM and read by the controller, `Total` and `Notes` loaded only) are sample imports. `expected.json` is written by hand and never generated by Proofmesh.
