# Provenance

Project-authored for Proofmesh. Apache-2.0. No third-party code, and none of `fastapi`, `flask`, `django` or `celery` is installed, so every route and task is recognised through the imports alone. A FastAPI app that includes an `APIRouter` with a prefix, which in turn includes a router imported under an alias; a router nothing includes (`api/admin.py`), taken as mounted at the root; `api_route` with two methods; a Starlette `{path:path}` parameter; and a route built from a module constant, which must stay unresolved. A Flask app that registers a blueprint under a `url_prefix` that replaces the blueprint's own, `route` with and without `methods`, `<int:...>` and `<path:...>` converters, a `bp.get` shortcut, and `methods` held in a variable, which must stay unresolved. A Django project whose `ROOT_URLCONF` includes an app's URLconf, a class-based view with `get` and `post`, one with no handler of its own, an `re_path` route left unresolved, and a URLconf nothing includes, which must stay unresolved. Celery tasks through `@app.task` on a `Celery` instance in another module, with and without arguments, and `@shared_task`. Decoys that must yield nothing: `@menu.get(...)` on an object of a workspace class, `@get(...)` imported from an unrelated package, and `@registry.task` on a non-Celery object. The expected facts in `expected.json` are written by hand.
